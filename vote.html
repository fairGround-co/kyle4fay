Early Voting Schedules and Locations

The Washington County Election Commission sets early voting dates, hours, and locations for every election. Any registered Washington County voter can early vote at any early voting site in the county.

Early voting schedules & locations →

Check Your Registration

Confirm you’re registered, find your polling place, and see a sample ballot at the Arkansas Secretary of State’s VoterView.

Bring Your ID

Arkansas requires voters to show photo identification when voting in person. If you don’t have one, you can cast a provisional ballot.

Questions?

Contact the Washington County Election Commission, or reach out to the campaign at kyle.smith@kyle4fay.org and we’ll help you find what you need.
