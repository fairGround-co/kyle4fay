How to Vote

Fayetteville City Council · Ward 4, Position 1

The Ward 4 Runoff

No one won a majority in the Ward 4 race on November 3, so the top two finishers meet in a runoff. If you’re a registered voter in Ward 4, this race is on your ballot.

  • Early voting begins: Tuesday, November 24
  • Runoff Election Day: Tuesday, December 1, polls open 7:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m.

Runoff early voting hours and locations can differ from November’s. Check with the county before you go.

Runoff voting hours & locations →

Check Your Registration

Not sure you’re in Ward 4? Confirm you’re registered, see your ward, find your polling place, and see a sample ballot at the Arkansas Secretary of State’s VoterView.

Bring Your ID

Arkansas requires voters to show photo identification when voting in person. If you don’t have one, you can cast a provisional ballot.

Questions?

Contact the Washington County Election Commission, or reach out to the campaign at kyle.smith@kyle4fay.org and we’ll help you find what you need.
