Endorsements

I’m grateful to the neighbors, community leaders, and organizations standing with this campaign. Want to add your name? Let me know.

Chad Hall

Former Ward 4 Candidate

After giving this decision a great deal of thought, I have decided to endorse Kyle Smith in the run-off election for Fayetteville City Council. I appreciate the work Teresa Turk has put into serving our city and Ward 4. There has been progress, and that work deserves recognition. At the same time, I believe Fayetteville has so much more to accomplish. We need to stay focused on where our city is going, the challenges ahead, and how we prepare for the future.

This election made it clear that there is a desire for a change in how Ward 4 is represented at City Hall. I believe Kyle and I are more closely aligned in our vision for Fayetteville and in the priorities we believe need continued attention.

Throughout my campaign, I focused on three issues that I believe are critical to Fayetteville’s future: affordable and attainable housing, addressing our aging infrastructure, and creating new jobs and opportunities for our residents. These aren’t issues that can be solved overnight, but they require leadership, thoughtful planning, and a willingness to move beyond simply maintaining the status quo. My hope is that Kyle can help carry these priorities forward through his work on the City Council and help turn those conversations into meaningful action.

For me, this is about more than an election. It is about making sure City Hall listens, responds, and works for the people it represents. It is about looking ahead, taking on the hard work in front of us, and building a Fayetteville where everyone has an opportunity to thrive.

I am hopeful that Kyle will bring that focus and commitment to the City Council. While my campaign may be over for now, my commitment to these issues and to the future of Fayetteville is not.

I wish both candidates the best and encourage everyone to vote in the run-off election.

Recognition

2026 Gun Sense Candidate

Moms Demand Action

From Kyle

I’m proud to share that my campaign has earned the 2026 Moms Demand Action Gun Sense Candidate distinction.

In September 2019, when I previously served on the Fayetteville City Council, a new Arkansas law limited our ability to keep guns out of city parks. The council voted 7-1 to change our city code to reflect the new state law. I cast the only “no” vote because I believed Fayetteville should be able to keep guns out of our parks and public spaces.

Whether we’re taking our kids to the park or spending an evening on Dickson Street, we should be able to enjoy Fayetteville without fear of gun violence.

If elected, I’ll keep speaking up for Fayetteville’s ability to make its own public safety decisions. And I’ll support common-sense ways to prevent gun violence and keep our community safe.

The Gun Sense Candidate distinction is not an endorsement from Moms Demand Action or Everytown for Gun Safety Action Fund.

Kyle is that rare gem, and I didn’t realize it until after the fact.

He showed up to all the various HOA sponsored activities and meetings, meetings I was chairing at the time and sometimes I didn’t want to be there! But he showed up.

In the following years, I have kept in touch with Kyle and am beyond thrilled that he is running to represent Ward 4 once again. He and I regularly talk about all the issues facing Fayetteville, from critical infrastructure (hello, working sewer lines!) to traffic concerns, zoning issues, etc., etc.

I am always amazed by his insight and thrilled that his views and values align so closely with mine. He is brilliant, driven, and genuine about his passion for both this city and the people he wants to represent again.

Kyle would be an excellent city councilmember, and he has my full support. I hope everyone will consider backing him as well!
